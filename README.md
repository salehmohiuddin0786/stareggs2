# Star Eggs — Premium 3D Minimal Website

An interactive, editorial, 3D luxury website for **Star Eggs**, wholesale fresh white egg supplier and bulk distributor serving **Telangana** and **Maharashtra corridors (Nagpur, Akola, Yavatmal, Arni, Vani)**.

---

## 3D Experience & Interactive Features

- **Real-Time 3D Egg Model:** High-fidelity mathematical ovoid geometry with realistic calcium porcelain shell physics, specular studio reflections, gentle levitation animation, and full 360° touch/mouse drag-to-rotate interaction.
- **Hero View Mode Switcher:** Toggle smoothly between the real-time interactive 3D model and editorial product photography.
- **Interactive 3D Shell Inspection Section:** Dedicated 3D quality inspection with acoustic resonance metrics, cuticle preservation details, and calibrated 50–55g weight specifications.
- **Live Color Palette Switcher (Preview Dock):** An interactive floating preview controller enabling instant switching between 4 luxury color palettes:
  1. *Warm Beige & Ivory (Brand Signature)*
  2. *Alabaster & Slate Noir (Minimalist Editorial)*
  3. *Golden Champagne & Espresso (Warm Amber Luxury)*
  4. *Emerald Pearl & Linen (Royal Botanical Accent)*

## Product Catalog (Fresh White Eggs Only)

1. **Grade-A Fresh White Table Eggs:** Daily harvest, pristine calcium shells, 50–55g weight class.
2. **Commercial 30-Egg Molded Trays:** Shock-dampened biodegradable fiber trays for daily grocery and restaurant use.
3. **Wholesale Master Crates (180 / 210 Count):** Heavy-duty reinforced transport packaging for long-haul inter-state logistics.

*(Note: Brown eggs have been removed in accordance with brand specifications).*

## Distribution Corridors

- **Maharashtra Hubs:** Nagpur (Logistics Terminal), Akola (Wholesale Depot), Yavatmal (Regional Market), Arni (Commercial Transit), Vani / Wani (Bulk Supply Depot).
- **Telangana Hubs:** Hyderabad & Secunderabad, Warangal & Hanamkonda, Nizamabad, Karimnagar, Adilabad, Khammam, Mahbubnagar.
